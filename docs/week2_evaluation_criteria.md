| **O3 / FR2 / NFR2** | Multi-Level Skill-Coverage Classification Correctness | Domain-specific capability | Evaluate SkillAlign-RAG and the single-pass RAG baseline against the curated ground-truth benchmark (3–5 SAIAH courses × 12–15 Malaysian AI skill tasks across **CLO**, **Taught**, and **Assessed** levels). | Per-level Macro-F1 score (derived from Precision and Recall); F1 delta vs. single-pass RAG baseline | Macro-F1 ≥ 0.80; positive F1 gain (\> 0.00) over single-pass RAG baseline | UTM lecturers and programme coordinators receive reliable, constructively aligned skill-coverage judgements across course outcomes, teaching slides, and assessments. |

| **O1 / FR1 / FR5** | Provenance Schema Completeness and Tabular Audit Clarity | Generation capability | Inspect ingested document chunks for provenance metadata preservation and evaluate generated side-by-side (**CLO**, **Taught**, **Assessed**) audit tables using a defined clarity rubric. | Provenance field completeness rate (`course_code`, `evidence_level`, `doc_id`, `page_or_slide_no`, `text_span`); clarity rubric score | 100% provenance metadata completeness; meets defined tabular clarity criterion | Programme coordinators can immediately interpret multi-level constructive alignment gaps in a standardized review table without manual cross-referencing. |

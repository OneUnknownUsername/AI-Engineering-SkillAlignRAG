# Section 0 — Project Framing & Application Scope Checkpoint

**Save in:** `docs/week2_requirements.md`

| Section 0 — Project Framing & Application Scope Checkpoint | Save in: `docs/week2_requirements.md` |
| :---- | :---- |
| **Problem Evidence** | **1\. Pedagogical, accreditation and national-policy evidence:** Constructive alignment holds that a course develops a target competency most effectively when its intended Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs), teaching activities (Taught) and assessments (Assessed) are coherently aligned ([Biggs, 1996](#ref-biggs-1996); [Biggs and Tang, 2011](#ref-biggs-tang-2011)), and MQA accreditation standards (*COPPA* and *GGP: Assessment of Student Learning*) expect programmes to align their outcomes, teaching and assessment ([Malaysian Qualifications Agency, 2017](#ref-mqa-2017), [2023](#ref-mqa-2023)). The *Malaysia Higher Education Blueprint 2026–2035* identifies the gap in future-ready skills as one of the main challenges facing higher learning institutions ([Ministry of Higher Education, 2026, p. 9](#ref-mohe-2026)), a mismatch that TalentCorp's workforce study also reports ([TalentCorp and KESUMA, 2024](#ref-talentcorp-2024)), while Enabler E2 of *AI Nation 2030* proposes leveraging AI tools to review the curriculum quality and skills coverage of accredited skilling programmes ([Ministry of Digital, 2026, p. 62](#ref-mod-2026)); this project applies that idea at university level. **2\. Operational evidence:** In the UTM SAIAH programme ([Faculty of Artificial Intelligence, n.d.](#ref-fai-nd)), intended outcomes (Course Information documents), weekly teaching content (lecture slides) and assessments (briefs, rubrics and examination papers) are spread across heterogeneous files. The three V1 courses hold 39 files and 1,411 pages, of which 11 are CI pages, 1,366 lecture-slide pages and 34 assessment pages ([Week 3 data inventory](week3_data_inventory.md)). Constructive alignment is already time-consuming to apply to a single course (Cobb, 2015, cited in [Lawrence, 2023](#ref-lawrence-2023)), and manual curriculum mapping is labour-intensive and prone to bias ([Jayalath et al., 2026](#ref-jayalath-2026)). |
| **Existing Approaches** | **1\. Manual and spreadsheet-based alignment review** ([Biggs and Tang, 2011](#ref-biggs-tang-2011); [Jayalath et al., 2026](#ref-jayalath-2026)): academic staff cross-check CLOs, weekly teaching plans and assessment rubrics during curriculum review. *What it already addresses:* direct ownership of the mapping and contextual judgement, but it is labour-intensive ([Jayalath et al., 2026](#ref-jayalath-2026)), hard to repeat across hundreds of slide pages and several file formats, and rarely records evidence at page level. **2\. Keyword, skills-based and embedding-based curriculum analytics** ([Manning, Raghavan and Schütze, 2008](#ref-manning-2008); [Kitto et al., 2020](#ref-kitto-2020); [Xu et al., 2025](#ref-xu-2025)): course text is mapped to skill taxonomies by keyword overlap, skill tagging or embedding similarity. *What it already addresses:* automated text matching, but keyword matching misses paraphrased evidence ([Karpukhin et al., 2020](#ref-karpukhin-2020)), a course is treated as one block of text ([Kitto et al., 2020](#ref-kitto-2020); [Xu et al., 2025](#ref-xu-2025)) so CLO, Taught and Assessed evidence is not checked separately, and the output is a score rather than a page-level citation. **3\. Single-pass RAG and LLM-based mapping** ([Lewis et al., 2020](#ref-lewis-2020); [Xu et al., 2025](#ref-xu-2025)): the model is grounded in top-k passages from one pooled index. *What it already addresses:* RAG outperformed TF-IDF, embeddings and zero-shot prompting ([Xu et al., 2025](#ref-xu-2025)), but evidence may be ranked too low or lost in a long context ([Barnett et al., 2024](#ref-barnett-2024); [Liu et al., 2024](#ref-liu-2024)), citations are not independently verified ([Liu, Zhang and Liang, 2023](#ref-liu-zhang-liang-2023)), and missing files are not separated from absent skills. |
| **Project / Engineering Gap** | **Practical & Technical Limitation** (a practical engineering gap, not a claim of research novelty): 1\. **Mixed-File Context Dilution:** The single-pass RAG baseline pools heterogeneous course files together (long lecture decks vs. concise Course Information documents and assessment rubrics), so top-k retrieval can rank the evidence for a specific constructive-alignment level (*CLO*, *Taught*, *Assessed*) too low to be retrieved ([Barnett et al., 2024](#ref-barnett-2024); [Liu et al., 2024](#ref-liu-2024)); in this corpus, CI documents are only 11 of 1,411 pages. 2\. **Lack of Deterministic Provenance & Epistemic Abstention:** Generated citations are often unsupported, as only 51.5% of sentences from generative search engines were fully supported by their citations ([Liu, Zhang and Liang, 2023](#ref-liu-zhang-liang-2023)). The baseline uses LLM-generated citations without deterministic substring matching or NLI entailment verification ([Gao et al., 2023](#ref-gao-2023); [Honovich et al., 2022](#ref-honovich-2022)), and does not explicitly distinguish **Insufficient Evidence** (required course document category missing or unprocessable) from **Not Found** (documents present, but no qualifying evidence supports the skill). |
| **Objective O1** | **To design** and construct a standardized, provenance-preserving curriculum evidence schema and annotated ground-truth dataset from **3** core UTM SAIAH courses (SAIA1013, SAIA2113, SAIA2143), using Course Information documents, lecture slides, and assessment rubrics in PDF/PPTX/DOCX format, paired with **12** curated HRD Corp IndSF-derived industry skill tasks. |
| **Objective O2** | **To develop** SkillAlign-RAG, a source-routed RAG pipeline for **CLO**, **Taught**, and **Assessed** evidence paired with deterministic text-span matching and NLI-based citation verification ([Gao et al., 2023](#ref-gao-2023); [Honovich et al., 2022](#ref-honovich-2022)) to classify skill coverage as **Supported**, **Not Found**, or **Insufficient Evidence**. |
| **Objective O3 (if relevant)** | **To evaluate** SkillAlign-RAG against a conventional single-pass RAG baseline using per-level Precision, Recall, and F1, citation faithfulness, unsupported-claim rate, end-to-end latency, and token usage. |
| **Intended Users** | University course lecturers, subject-matter experts, and programme coordinators teaching or overseeing core courses in the UTM Bachelor of Artificial Intelligence with Honours (SAIAH) programme ([Faculty of Artificial Intelligence, n.d.](#ref-fai-nd)). |
| **Supported Tasks** | 1. Allow approved academic staff to sign in and upload PDF/PPTX/DOCX course files by CLO, Taught, and Assessed category, with per-file processing feedback and preserved provenance. 2. Show the 12 predefined HRD Corp IndSF-derived skills without industry-input setup. 3. Present all 36 course–skill combinations and 108 coverage cells in an interactive dashboard, with course/skill filters and Pending/Processing states. 4. Show Supported, Not Found, or Insufficient Evidence with inspectable verified quotations and exact source references. 5. Treat CI as authoritative for CLO; label matching slide corroboration and display conflicts. No free-text question interface is provided. |
| **Information / Knowledge Domain** | Higher-education curriculum quality assurance and Outcome-Based Education (OBE) for undergraduate AI curricula ([Malaysian Qualifications Agency, 2017](#ref-mqa-2017), [2023](#ref-mqa-2023); [Biggs, 1996](#ref-biggs-1996)), covering the three V1 SAIAH course packages and 12 curated skill tasks grounded in HRD Corp IndSF Data Science and Artificial Intelligence documents ([Human Resource Development Corporation, 2023](#ref-hrdc-2023), [n.d.](#ref-hrdc-nd)). The policy and workforce references above establish project context; they are not additional V1 benchmark inputs. |
| **Operating Context** | Interactive curriculum-review and evidence-audit dashboard used by academic staff during course preparation, Continual Quality Improvement (CQI) reporting, and MQA programme accreditation preparation ([Malaysian Qualifications Agency, 2017](#ref-mqa-2017)), the dashboard and its backend run on the university B200 GPU node next to model inference (accessed through Open OnDemand) and are viewed in a laptop browser through the developer's VS Code tunnel port forwarding; lecturers see the dashboard in on-screen demonstrations. |
| **Important Constraints** | • **Confidentiality & Privacy:** Internal UTM course files and unpublished rubrics must not be exposed to unauthorized external training APIs; prioritize local open-weight inference or sanitized, zero-retention API calls. • **Dataset Scope:** Bounded V1 prototype of 3 SAIAH courses and 12 curated HRD Corp IndSF-derived skill tasks. • **Compute & Budget:** Use the university B200 GPU allocation for local model inference, accessed through Open OnDemand. The laptop browser is the demonstration interface and is not required to run the models; keep API inference cost below **US\$0.50 per 100 skill-audit queries**. • **Timeline:** Prototype development and evaluation must be completed within the semester lab schedule. |
| **Explicit Out-of-Scope Tasks** | • Shall not provide features to approve, rewrite, or submit official MQA accreditation documents (`MQA-01`/`MQA-02`) or university Senate curriculum revision papers ([Malaysian Qualifications Agency, 2017](#ref-mqa-2017)). • Shall not grade student work, evaluate individual lecturer teaching performance, or process student personal data (PII). • Shall not infer skill coverage from unverified external web sources or general LLM parametric knowledge outside the uploaded course corpus. • Shall not operate as a general-purpose student advising or open-domain Q\&A chatbot. |
| **Evaluation Focus** | Determine whether SkillAlign-RAG improves **per-level Precision, Recall, and F1** over a conventional single-pass RAG baseline; achieves **≥95% citation faithfulness** and **≤5% unsupported-claim rate** on the curated ground-truth benchmark (proposed engineering acceptance criteria); accurately separates **Not Found** from **Insufficient Evidence**; and satisfies interactive latency (\< 3,000 ms per level / target workload) and token-budget (\< US\$0.50 per 100 queries) thresholds. |

## Section 1 — Functional Requirements (FR)

| \# | Requirement — The system SHALL | Scope Boundary — SHALL NOT (where relevant) |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **FR1** | Provide authorised academic staff with upload controls for Course Information documents (**CLO**), lecture slide decks (**Taught**), and assessment rubrics (**Assessed**) in PDF, PPTX, or DOCX format for a selected course, and a **Submit** button to start automatic processing. Display each submitted file's processing outcome and list successfully processed documents with their course code and evidence category. For unsupported formats or processing failures, display an alert identifying the affected file and the reason; for unsupported formats, list the accepted formats. Require staff to confirm that the files are approved course materials and contain no student submissions before submission. | Accept unsupported file formats or files without an identified course and evidence category; provide external-web ingestion; treat the staff declaration as automatic detection of student submissions. |
| **FR2** | Display, by default, an audit overview containing all three supported SAIAH courses and all 12 curated industry-skill benchmark tasks across **CLO**, **Taught**, and **Assessed** (36 course–skill rows and 108 coverage cells). Allow users to filter by course, skill, or both and clear filters to restore the full overview. For each completed coverage cell, display **Supported** (qualifying verified evidence found), **Not Found** (the required category was processed but no qualifying evidence was found), or **Insufficient Evidence** (the required category is missing or could not be processed), with the reason for Insufficient Evidence. Display **Pending** or **Processing** for unfinished cells. | Present an unfinished audit as a completed coverage classification; conflate **Insufficient Evidence** with **Not Found**; describe **Not Found** as proof that a course lacks the skill; infer coverage from general model knowledge outside the uploaded course corpus. |
| **FR3** | Provide at least one verbatim supporting text span (`text_span`) together with its exact source document identifier and page or slide reference (`doc_id`, `page_or_slide_no`) for every evidence level classified as **Supported**. For CLO results, show the authoritative Course Information (CI) evidence. Where lecture slides are cited as corroborating CLO evidence, also show the corresponding CI statement and identify the slide citation as corroboration. If the sources conflict, base the CLO classification on the CI document and display a conflict notice identifying the differing statements and their source references. | Present fabricated, unverified, or non-verbatim quotations; use lecture slides as the sole basis for CLO support or override conflicting CI evidence; use another evidence category to substantiate Taught or Assessed coverage. |
| **FR4** | Restrict audit selections to the three supported SAIAH courses and the 12 curated benchmark skills, and display a clear scope message when an unsupported course or skill selection is submitted. | Provide a free-text query interface, automatically approve, rewrite, or submit official accreditation documents (`MQA-01`/`MQA-02`) or Senate curriculum papers, grade student work, evaluate individual lecturer performance, or provide general student advising. |
| **FR5** | Present skill-coverage audit results in an interactive dashboard with **CLO**, **Taught**, and **Assessed** classifications arranged side by side for curriculum review and Continual Quality Improvement (CQI) inspection. Allow users to inspect the supporting quotations and exact document/page or slide references for each **Supported** result. | \- |
| **FR6** | Provide a sign-in page and allow course-document uploads only for authenticated accounts assigned an approved academic-staff role. Display an access-denied message when an unauthenticated user or an account without that role attempts to upload files. | Allow self-registration or successful sign-in alone to grant upload privileges; accept unauthorised uploads through either the interface or direct backend requests. |
| **FR7** | Present the 12 predefined curated industry-skill benchmark tasks, including their identifiers, names, descriptions, and HRD Corp IndSF source references, when users open the dashboard, without requiring users to upload industry documents or configure the benchmark. | Require an industry-document upload, configuration step, or load button before users can view the predefined skill benchmark. |

The V1 supported courses are **SAIA1013**, **SAIA2113**, and **SAIA2143**; the curated benchmark contains **S01–S12**. HRD Corp IndSF documents are the default industry source; the 12 curated tasks derived from them define the audit benchmark. Automatic source loading and Pipeline A/Pipeline B execution are implementation details; the requirements above specify the interactions and visible outcomes.

**CLO authority policy:** CI documents govern CLO classifications. Slides may corroborate explicit CLO statements recorded in CI documents but cannot introduce an additional CLO commitment. Missing or unprocessable CI evidence produces **Insufficient Evidence** for CLO even if slides contain CLO statements. A conflict notice requires differing source statements and references, rather than an assumption that an unmentioned skill agrees with CI.

**Upload-content policy:** Only approved course materials may be submitted. FR6 controls who may upload; the FR1 declaration records the uploader's confirmation of acceptable content. Neither control establishes automatic semantic detection of student submissions.

**Processing lifecycle:** Submitting course files starts Pipeline A, index updates,
and recalculation of affected audit cells without additional user-run commands.
Affected old results must not be presented as current while updates are pending.
Dashboard filters operate on saved results and do not trigger new inference.
Missing or wholly unprocessable categories produce Insufficient Evidence;
partially processed categories must disclose file failures. Model/generation
errors must be shown as failures rather than silently relabelled Not Found.

**Measurement units:** One skill audit means one course–skill pair across all
three evidence levels. The full V1 refresh contains 36 such audits (108 cells).
NFR1 measures inference for an individual level and a full three-level audit;
it does not specify that all 108 cells finish within nine seconds. NFR3's budget
is per 100 full skill audits (300 evidence-level cells). Dashboard loading and
filtering times must be reported separately from inference time; model startup
and document processing time must also be identified separately in evaluation.

## Section 3 — Responsible AI Requirements (RAI)

| \# | Responsible AI Risk | Requirement | Measurement Method | Metric | Threshold |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **RAI1** | Transparency / Grounding (Hallucinated or Unverified Citations) | The system shall provide a verifiable verbatim text span and exact source reference (`doc_id`, `page_or_slide_no`) for every **Supported** skill-coverage claim, validated via deterministic substring matching and NLI entailment verification. | Audit all **Supported** responses across the evaluation benchmark using automated substring checks, NLI entailment scoring, and manual verification against source PDFs/PPTXs. | Citation presence rate; citation faithfulness rate; unsupported-claim rate | 100% citation & verbatim-span presence; ≥ 95% citation faithfulness; ≤ 5% unsupported-claim rate |
| **RAI2** | Epistemic Uncertainty & Scope Safety (Unfair Course Evaluation / Out-of-Scope Use) | The system shall distinguish **Insufficient Evidence** (required document category missing or unprocessable) from **Not Found** (required category processed but no qualifying evidence found), show unfinished audits as Pending or Processing, and restrict audits to supported course and benchmark-skill selections. | Run controlled missing-category, wholly unprocessable-category, processed-without-qualifying-evidence, and unfinished-audit cases. Attempt at least 20 unsupported course/skill selections through manipulated interface and direct backend requests. Inspect the interface for the absence of free-text query, grading, lecturer-evaluation, and accreditation-approval features. | Coverage-state handling accuracy; unsupported-selection rejection rate; number of excluded task features exposed | 100% correct handling of prepared coverage-state cases; 100% unsupported-selection rejection; 0 excluded task features exposed |
| **RAI3** | Privacy / Confidentiality (Exposure of UTM Course Files & PII) | The system shall not transmit unpublished exam keys or staff/student personal data (PII) to unauthorized external model APIs. | Run privacy and confidentiality test cases containing synthetic staff/student PII and restricted assessment markers while inspecting outgoing network/API payloads and logs. | Number of detected unauthorized PII or restricted-document transmissions | 0 unauthorized transmissions |

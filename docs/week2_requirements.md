# Section 0 — Project Framing & Application Scope Checkpoint

| Section 0 — Project Framing & Application Scope Checkpoint | Save in: `docs/week2_requirements.md` |
| :---- | :---- |
| **Problem Evidence** | **1\. National Policy, Accreditation & Workforce Evidence:** Malaysia's *National AI Action Plan 2026–2030 (AI Nation 2030\)* (Foundational Enabler E2, p. 62\) explicitly calls for *"leveraging AI tools to review curriculum quality and skills coverage"* across higher education ([A1](#ref-a1)), alongside MOHE's national AI talent roadmap ([A6](#ref-a6)). **Malaysian Accreditation & Workforce Context:** MQA accreditation standards (*COPPA* and *GGP: Assessment of Student Learning*) and UTM CDex curriculum review guidelines require strict constructive alignment across Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs), weekly teaching materials, and assessments ([A2](#ref-a2), [A3](#ref-a3), [A7](#ref-a7)), while TalentCorp's national workforce study highlights persistent curriculum-to-industry skill mismatches in Malaysia ([A4](#ref-a4)). **2\. Pedagogical & Operational Evidence:** In the UTM SAIAH programme ([A14](#ref-a14)), intended outcomes (Course Information PDFs), weekly teaching content (lecture PPTXs), and assessments (rubrics) are distributed across heterogeneous files with different granularity, making manual constructive-alignment verification slow and difficult to audit consistently ([A8](#ref-a8), [A9](#ref-a9)). |
| **Existing Approaches** | **1\. Manual OBE / Constructive-Alignment Reviews:** Academic staff manually cross-check CLOs, weekly teaching schedules, and assessment rubrics during curriculum review cycles ([A2](#ref-a2), [A7](#ref-a7), [A8](#ref-a8)). *What it already addresses:* Provides thorough contextual and pedagogical judgement, but is slow, labour-intensive, and hard to scale across granular lecture slides and rubrics. **2\. Lexical Search & Classical NLP Outcome Mapping:** Keyword search ([A15](#ref-a15)) and embedding cosine-similarity pipelines ([A13](#ref-a13)) map course outcomes to programme outcomes or skill taxonomies. *What it already addresses:* Automates surface-level text matching, but struggles with semantic mismatch between academic phrasing and industry skill terminology and does not verify multi-level evidence across syllabi, slides, and rubrics. **3\. Conventional Single-Pass RAG & LLM Assistants:** Standard RAG pipelines retrieve top-k passages from a single pooled index to answer document queries ([A10](#ref-a10)). *What it already addresses:* Enables document-grounded synthesis, but pooling heterogeneous course files causes context dilution, conflates missing files with skill absence, and suffers from unverified or unsupported citations without external entailment checks ([A11](#ref-a11), [A12](#ref-a12)). |
| **Project / Engineering Gap** | **Practical & Technical Limitation:** 1\. **Mixed-File Context Dilution:** The proposed single-pass RAG baseline pools heterogeneous course files together (verbose lecture PPTXs vs. concise Course Information PDFs and assessment rubrics), which can cause top-k retrieval to miss evidence at specific constructive-alignment levels (*CLO*, *Taught*, *Assessed*). 2\. **Lack of Deterministic Provenance & Epistemic Abstention:** The proposed baseline uses LLM-generated citations without deterministic substring matching or NLI entailment verification ([A11](#ref-a11), [A12](#ref-a12)), and does not explicitly distinguish **Insufficient Evidence** (required course document type missing from the repository) from **Not Found** (documents present, but no qualifying evidence supports the skill). |
| **Objective O1** | **To design** and construct a standardized, provenance-preserving curriculum evidence schema and annotated ground-truth dataset from **3–5** core UTM SAIAH courses (Course Information PDFs, lecture PPTXs, and assessment rubrics) paired with **12–15** Malaysian AI industry skill tasks. |
| **Objective O2** | **To develop** SkillAlign-RAG, a source-routed RAG pipeline for **CLO**, **Taught**, and **Assessed** evidence paired with deterministic text-span matching and NLI-based citation verification ([A11](#ref-a11), [A12](#ref-a12)) to classify skill coverage as **Supported**, **Not Found**, or **Insufficient Evidence**. |
| **Objective O3 (if relevant)** | **To evaluate** SkillAlign-RAG against a conventional single-pass RAG baseline using per-level Precision, Recall, and F1, citation faithfulness, unsupported-claim rate, end-to-end latency, and token usage. |
| **Intended Users** | University course lecturers, subject-matter experts, and programme coordinators teaching or overseeing core courses in the UTM Bachelor of Artificial Intelligence with Honours (SAIAH) programme ([A14](#ref-a14)). |
| **Supported Tasks** | 1\. Ingest Course Information PDFs, lecture PPTXs, and assessment rubrics while preserving `course_code`, `evidence_level`, `doc_id`, `page_or_slide_no`, and `text_span`. 2\. Retrieve multi-level course evidence for a target industry AI skill separately across **CLO**, **Taught**, and **Assessed** document stores. 3\. Verify candidate evidence quotes using deterministic text-span matching and an NLI entailment verifier ([A11](#ref-a11), [A12](#ref-a12)). 4\. Present a structured tabular skill-coverage audit classifying each level as **Supported** (with verbatim quote and exact page/slide citation), **Not Found** (documents available, no qualifying evidence found), or **Insufficient Evidence** (required document category missing). |
| **Information / Knowledge Domain** | Higher-education curriculum quality assurance and Outcome-Based Education (OBE) for undergraduate AI curricula ([A2](#ref-a2), [A3](#ref-a3), [A8](#ref-a8)), covering 3–5 core UTM SAIAH course packages and a curated benchmark of 12–15 Malaysian AI industry skill tasks derived from *AI Nation 2030*, TalentCorp/MyMAHIR, MDEC frameworks, and Malaysian AI job descriptions ([A1](#ref-a1), [A4](#ref-a4), [A5](#ref-a5)). |
| **Operating Context** | Interactive curriculum-review and evidence-audit dashboard used by academic staff during course preparation, Continual Quality Improvement (CQI) reporting, and MQA programme accreditation preparation ([A2](#ref-a2), [A7](#ref-a7)), running on Google Colab or a local workstation in a controlled academic environment. |
| **Important Constraints** | • **Confidentiality & Privacy:** Internal UTM course files and unpublished rubrics must not be exposed to unauthorized external training APIs; prioritize local open-weight inference or sanitized, zero-retention API calls. • **Dataset Scope:** Bounded V1 prototype of 3–5 SAIAH courses and 12–15 Malaysian industry AI skill tasks. • **Compute & Budget:** Must run within free/student-tier Google Colab GPU resources (e.g., T4 GPU) and keep API inference cost below **US\$0.50 per 100 skill-audit queries**. • **Timeline:** Prototype development and evaluation must be completed within the semester lab schedule. |
| **Explicit Out-of-Scope Tasks** | • Shall not automatically approve, rewrite, or submit official MQA accreditation documents (`MQA-01`/`MQA-02`) or university Senate curriculum revision papers without human academic oversight ([A2](#ref-a2), [A7](#ref-a7)). • Shall not grade student work, evaluate individual lecturer teaching performance, or process student personal data (PII). • Shall not infer skill coverage from unverified external web sources or general LLM parametric knowledge outside the uploaded course corpus. • Shall not operate as a general-purpose student advising or open-domain Q\&A chatbot. |
| **Evaluation Focus** | Determine whether SkillAlign-RAG improves **per-level Precision, Recall, and F1** over a conventional single-pass RAG baseline; achieves **≥95% citation faithfulness** and **≤5% unsupported-claim rate** on the curated ground-truth benchmark (proposed engineering acceptance criteria); accurately separates **Not Found** from **Insufficient Evidence**; and satisfies interactive latency (\< 3,000 ms per level / target workload) and token-budget (\< US\$0.50 per 100 queries) thresholds. |
